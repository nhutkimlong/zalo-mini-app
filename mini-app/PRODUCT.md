# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Pilgrims, hikers, and tourists visiting Mount Bà Đen (Tây Ninh, Vietnam). They are often on the move, outdoors in bright sunlight, navigating trails, cable cars, or temples. They need immediate access to maps, emergency hotlines, live weather updates, and audio guides, along with an interactive travel diary (gamification badges).

## Product Purpose
The Mount Bà Đen Tourism Assistant (Zalo Mini App) is a pocket companion that guides visitors through the cultural, spiritual, and physical experience of the mountain. Success means visitors can navigate safely, learn about the sacred heritage, track their journey stamps, and get instant answers via the built-in AI chatbot without feeling overwhelmed by complex interfaces.

## Brand Personality
- **Spiritual**: Respectful of the sacred heritage of Black Lady Mountain.
- **Majestic**: Evoking the awe of the mountain peaks through gold accents and deep navy surfaces.
- **Tactile**: Reassuring and responsive to every physical touch on mobile screens.

## Anti-references
- **Generic SaaS Dashboards**: Multi-colored analytics charts, flat gray-on-white card blocks, and layout template clichés.
- **AI Purple/Indigo Slop**: Banned purple/violet tones and neon mesh gradients.
- **Layout Thrashing**: Bouncing elements, sudden height shifts, or laggy layout-shifting animations.

## Design Principles
1. **Sacred Gold & Navy Narrative**: Elevate the visual identity using a cohesive dark-and-light-theme friendly golden scale against solid navy bases.
2. **Context-Aware Tactile Feedback**: Every interaction must feel responsive and physically reassuring with scale transformations, avoiding generic hover/active behaviors.
3. **GPU-Accelerated Smoothness**: No layout shifts on transition (such as max-height/width). All motion uses transform/opacity to run smoothly at 60fps on mobile.
4. **Resilient Outlines**: Ensure focus rings are high-contrast and easy to trigger for physical accessibility in high-glare mountain environments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **WCAG AA Compliance**: Ensure high text contrast against backgrounds, readable typography, and explicit focus-visible states.
- **Touch Target Targets**: Maintain 36px-44px targets on mobile, allowing comfortable tap actions.
- **Prefers-Reduced-Motion Support**: Provide instant states or simple crossfades when reduced motion is requested.
